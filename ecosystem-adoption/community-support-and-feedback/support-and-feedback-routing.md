# Support and Feedback Routing

## Decision

Reproducible product defects and contribution work use the public repository;
ideas and use-case feedback use community discussion channels. Security
vulnerabilities are reported privately, and contracted commercial support is a
separate path from community help.

## Rationale

Public technical discussion makes ordinary problems searchable and lets the
community collaborate on evidence. Sensitive vulnerability details require a
private channel, while commercial engagements need expectations that public
community channels do not establish.

## Constraints

- Public defect reports should include enough context to reproduce and
  evaluate the problem.
- Vulnerability details must not be posted in public issue or discussion
  channels.
- Feedback intake does not itself create a roadmap commitment, response-time
  guarantee, or support entitlement.
- Channel activity and individual conversations remain in their source
  systems rather than becoming canonical Tree content.
