# Community Conduct

## Decision

Issues, reviews, documentation, and code contributions are governed by the
project Code of Conduct and a reciprocal expectation of respectful,
transparent collaboration.

## Rationale

Technical review is a shared learning and decision process. Respectful
discussion makes it safe to surface uncertainty, enables contributors to ask
for help, and preserves the community capacity on which sustained maintenance
depends.

## Constraints

- Technical merit does not exempt a contribution from community conduct
  requirements.
- Contributors disclose uncertainty and engage with review rather than acting
  as an unexplained pass-through for generated work.
- Maintainers and contributors reciprocate respect while working toward
  consensus and a reviewable result.
